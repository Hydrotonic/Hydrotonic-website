From the field

Bihar hairy caterpillar

Spilarctia obliqua

  1. DiagnoseYoung caterpillars feed in groups on the underside of leaves and leave them thin and papery. Older ones grow long orange-brown hairs, spread out and eat whole leaves. It attacks leafy greens, vegetables and pulses.
  2. ManageRemove egg masses and leaves carrying young caterpillars before they spread, wearing gloves because the hairs irritate skin. If numbers build up, we advise neem-based or biological sprays that suit food crops, and when it is safe to harvest.
  3. OptimiseCheck the undersides of leaves every week, more often after the rains when it is most active, and keep weeds around the towers down so it has fewer places to breed.
