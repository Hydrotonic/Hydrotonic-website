About Hydrotonic

We aim to make starting and expanding a hydroponic farm simpler and cheaper, so more people can make better use of the space they have and grow fresh food year-round.

Watch: 1,500 plants in 2 hours

Why Hydrotonic

Hydrotonic is an agricultural technology startup founded in 2025 in Belagavi by Mohammed Daanish Mulla, an agriculture graduate (B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, UAS Dharwad).

  • Founded and run by an agriculture graduate
  • Towers designed and manufactured in-house
  • Systems, supplies and support from one place
  • Sized to your space and budget
