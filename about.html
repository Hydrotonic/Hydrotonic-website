Why Hydrotonic

Hydrotonic is an agricultural technology startup founded in 2025 in Belagavi by Mohammed Daanish Mulla, an agriculture graduate (B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture, UAS Dharwad).

Mohammed Daanish Mulla

Mohammed Daanish Mulla

Founder, Hydrotonic

  • Education

    B.Sc. (Hons.) Agriculture from the College of Agriculture, University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS), Dharwad.

  • Fieldwork with farmers

    Rural Agricultural Work Experience (RAWE) in Shirahatti taluk, Gadag district: prepared farmer awareness charts and demonstration models on pest traps, government schemes, storage pest management, neem seed kernel extract and biochar.

  • Founded Hydrotonic
