Privacy Notice

Last updated: 22 October 2026. This notice is available in English.

Hydrotonic ("we", "us") runs hydrotonic.org. This notice explains what personal data we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Enquiry form: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, the product or service you are interested in, and anything you write in the message box.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: whatever you choose to share when you contact us directly.
  • Your preferences: your chosen language and light or dark mode are saved in your own browser (local storage). They are not sent to us.

We do not use analytics, advertising trackers or our own cookies on this website.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and follow up about the products or services you asked about. We do not sell your data or use it for unrelated marketing.

Your consent

The enquiry form asks for your consent before you send it. You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing us, and we will stop contacting you and delete your enquiry unless the law requires us to keep it.

Services that handle your data

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts our email inbox.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) handles messages if you contact us there.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash serve the fonts and some photos, so your browser connects to them when the page loads.
  • Instagram (Meta) posts in our gallery load only if you click "Show Instagram posts". Instagram may then set its own cookies.

Some of these providers may process data outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to respond and to serve you as a customer, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you, correct it, or erase it. You can also raise a complaint with us, and if you are not satisfied with our response, with the Data Protection Board of India.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email hydrotonichydroponics@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
