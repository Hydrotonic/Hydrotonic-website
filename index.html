Tower highlights

Built to grow up, not out

  1. Vertical design

    Many plants on a small footprint, so a terrace or rooftop becomes a farm.

  2. Recirculating water

    A pump lifts nutrient water from the reservoir to the top, and it trickles back down past the roots.

  3. Soil-free growing

FAQ

Common questions

What is a hydroponic tower?

A vertical column where plants grow without soil. A pump circulates water mixed with nutrients past the roots, so many plants fit in a small footprint.

How much space do I need?

Towers grow upward, so a balcony, terrace or rooftop is often enough to start. For commercial farms we plan the layout around your site.

What can I grow?

Leafy greens and herbs grow best: lettuce, spinach, basil, mint, coriander and microgreens. Ask us about other crops for your setup.

How much does a system cost?

It depends on the number of towers and the setup. Send us your space and goals on WhatsApp and we'll share a quote.

Is it hard to maintain?

Mostly you check the water level and nutrients regularly. We guide you through setup and your first crops, and stay available for questions.

Do you supply nutrients and accessories?

Yes: nutrient solutions, cocopeat, net pots, seedling trays, pumps and fittings.

Do you set up commercial farms?

Yes. We design and set up hydroponic facilities for businesses and commercial growers.
